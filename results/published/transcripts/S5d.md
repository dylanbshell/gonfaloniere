A widow's grief and a friar's habit are my armour, but I must know how much he truly holds before I decide whether to charm him or buy him."
  Contessa Isabella SAYS: "A table? Messer Bartolo, I have buried a husband and given my hours to the friars; if I kept a table it was for the poor of San Frediano, not for gamblers. If you mean to drag a penitent widow before the city for a thing seven years buried, say so now, plainly."

If he means to carry this to LeNoir or to the city, I must know it now and find my defence before the Murder Round."

Now tell me what you mean to do with this, Messer Bartolo, for I would rather know my judge than guess at him."
