A handsome beast it is, and it has guarded that doorway longer than either of us has breathed. But come, Messer Bartolo, you are a man of the Calimala; tell me instead how the cloth trade fares, for I hear the French buy wool by the cartload."

For my part I lost more there than I care to name, and gave the rest to the friars. A widow's purse is lighter, but her conscience sleeps better."
